## what's going on?

This is intended as a blog to document some of the areas of AI that I've played with over the last year or so, what I've learned from them and document future work too.

Part of my IRL job is to help with designing, testing and implementing digital and AI solutions to a large group of non-technical people within a large (100+ people) tax team. Some of my ramblings may touch on things learned in that role either hands-on or through discussions or training with others. This background also informs the basis for a lot of my experimentation which tends to sit in the world of UK tax, as an underlying expertise from my time in practice.

Whilst I may use AI to help with the design and functionality of this blog, the words you read will be my own.

Nothing on this blog is tax or accounting advice. All opinions my own.
